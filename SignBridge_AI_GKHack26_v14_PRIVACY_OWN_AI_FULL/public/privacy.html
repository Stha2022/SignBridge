SignBridge AI — Privacy Notice

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Privacy by design: SignBridge is designed to minimise personal information and avoid retaining raw camera footage or conversation content by default.

What we collect

When an account is created, SignBridge may collect information such as your name, email address, password hash, communication preferences and optional emergency-contact information. Only information needed for the selected service should be provided.

Camera and conversations

Camera access is permission-based. The production design is intended to process recognition in the browser/device and not retain raw camera footage by default. Conversation content is not intended to be used as AI training data unless a separate, explicit consent process is introduced.

AI training data

Production-user information is kept separate from any consented dataset used to improve the SignBridge recognition model. A user does not automatically become a training-data source by using the application.

Security

SignBridge uses measures such as password hashing, authenticated sessions, input validation, prepared database statements where applicable, HTTPS for deployed services, and data minimisation. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

Consent should be specific and understandable. Optional communications should not be bundled into essential privacy consent. Users should be able to contact the service owner about questions concerning their information.

Important

This prototype notice is not legal advice and should be reviewed against the final production architecture and applicable POPIA requirements before commercial deployment.
