# SignBridge AI — Competitor / Reference Research

## NID SASL Video Dictionary

NID's SASL Video Dictionary is a major South African reference resource. Its Google Play listing describes a bilingual English/SASL video dictionary with search by English word, handshape and body location, community variations, and standardised signs approved by PanSALB. The listing also reports a beginner SASL course and a K53 learner's-licence course in SASL.

NID's 2025 annual report says its updated dictionary contains more than 12,000 signs, clearer videos, SASL games and easier navigation.

### What SignBridge can learn from this
- Provide learning resources, not only translation.
- Respect regional/community variation.
- Make signs contextual rather than mapping one English word to one gesture.
- Validate the vocabulary with SASL users and relevant experts.
- Link users to trusted learning resources where appropriate rather than claiming SignBridge is the authoritative dictionary.

## Signapse

Signapse currently provides AI sign-language products for BSL and ASL, including text-to-sign video, website translation, API integration and transport/accessibility use cases. Its current products include SignStream Chat, SignStream API and SignStudio.

### What SignBridge can learn from this
- Make text-to-sign a core experience.
- Build an integration/API path for institutional customers.
- Design for real-world deployments such as public services and digital screens.
- Keep the architecture extensible to additional sign languages.
- Do not claim universal sign-language translation; language-specific models need language-specific training and validation.

## Current SignBridge positioning
SignBridge starts with South African Sign Language and a focused, community-validated vocabulary. It combines two-way communication, mobile-first access, multilingual UI, offline-capable design and a learning component. The current recognition model is a prototype and must be validated before production accuracy claims are made.
